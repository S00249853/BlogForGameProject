Week 1

Week 1 was mainly spent setting up the project. I set up a 3d unity project with a basic player character with simple movement (Walk, Jump, Dash) based off of a previous project.

Week 2

During Week 2 I started updating the player characters movement. I changed how the character jumped to ensure it would stay consistent no matter the framerate, and added triple jumping. As well as this, i started working on sideflipping and wall jumping.

Week 3

During Week 3 I continued updating the player characters movement. To start I added sideflipping and wall jumping, although the wall jump needs work. Then, I re-did the players camera to work with cinemachine. I then started worked on making the player move in the direction that the camera is facing, as well as this, I re-did the players movement to make it easier to understand and update.

Week 4

During Week 4 I added a hierarchical state machine for the player, and began refactoring my old player script so that everything works the same as it did. This will make it easier to update the player, and to fix bugs in the future.

Week 5

During Week 5 I fully refactored my player to work with the new hierarchical state machine, I also added the ability to ground pound, slide on walls and wall jump. As well as this, I added two buttons, one to destroy certain objects and one that spawns a timed platforming challenge, that can be activated by jumping on them. Then, I started working on a basic enemy, by adding a basic state machine, and logic for chasing the player.

Week 6

During Week 6 I first finished the logic for the enemys state machine, giving it a set of points to patrol between, and making it chase the player when it gets close enough. After this, I added player knockback, as well as invincibility frames after it gets hit. I also gave the player the ability to kill an enemy by jumping on its head, after which the player will bounce in the air, with the logic for this being usable for any other bouncy surfaces, i.e. springs.

Week 7

During Week 7 I first cleaned up the players state machine to ensure that each state transitioned between each other correctly, and cleanly. Then, I started working on adding moving platforms, and allowing the player to move with them, although I haven't managed to get that working yet. I then added coyote time, allowing the player to jump briefly after they walk off the edge of a platform, and a jump buffer, so if the player presses jump moments before they hit the ground, they will jump when they land. I also changed how triple jumps work. Now, the player needs to constantly be moving to be able to triple jump.

Week 8

During Week 8 I started working on the level design. To start, I added cannons and springs to be used throughout the game. I then made templates for the exterior and interior of a tower. the interior will be used for the tutorial, as well as for any other smaller levels I add to the game, and the exterior will be used for decorations in the main level. I then made a tutorial level, using the previously made template. It is a short level that makes the player use most of the games movement mechanics, as well as introducing them to some of the games obstacles, i.e. cannons and enemies. While making the level, I added blocks that can be destroyed by ground pounding them, and a new type of button that reveals hidden objects/platforms when pressed. I also made a new prefab for an archway to handle scene transitions, and started working on scene transitions. I also cleaned up some issues with the games prefabs.

Week 9

During Week 9 I implemented a climbing mechanic, allowing players to climb on certain walls. I also started planning, and designing the games main level, by first making an empty hub.

Week 10

During Week 10 I continued working on the main level, I first made a small climbing challenge, followed by a section with springs. I then added a checkpoint system, as well as teleporters, although the checkpoint system doesn't work properly yet. I also added a system for level transitions, making the scene fade out when transitioning to a new level, and ensuring the player spawns in where the transition they enter leads to when they enter a new scene.

Week 11

During Week 11 I first fixed the issue with the checkpoints and teleporters from last week. I then added some basic UI Elements. I then added a persistent game manager class to keep data like player health and collected collectables consistent across scenes, and to handle the UI This class also handles respawning the player when they run out of health. I refactored the players class to make checkpoints be handled by the game manager, as well as the scene swapper class. I also continued working on the main level.

Week 12

During Week 12 I continued working on the main level. This week, I added a hidden island off the main path in the level, although for now i'm only working on the path to reach it. I also added spheres allowing the player to dash again if they are dashed into, as well as boundaries that kill the player if touched. I also fixed the issues with the moving platform, and climbing. I also added scriptable objects for the bounciness of the springs, to keep the springs bounciness consistent with each other.

Week 13

I started Week 13 by cleaning up some issues with the path up to the hidden island. I then added a hidden area underneath the hub area, with a climbing challenge that is missing some details, and added platforms that dissapear and reappear at intervals. Then, I fixed some issues with wall-jumping, triple jumping and falling, and started working on a small level in a different scene that uses the dissapearing platforms.

Week 14

To start Week 14, I fixed an issue with the wall-jumps rotation, where the player would face a set direction in the time after jumping where the player is unable to move. I then finished working on the shorter level I started last week. Then, I added small hidden platforming challenges on the sides of the two towers in the hub area, with an extra challenge at the top of both towers. One of them focuses on moving platforms, while the other focuses on precise platforming on narrow surfaces. Then, I added four small platforming challenges to the hub area, although some of them may need to be tweaked a bit. I then made an outline for the last part of the main level, and continued working on the hidden island. I then started working on a second shorter level, this one focusing on dashing.

Week 15

During Week 15, I first finished the second short level. I then added details to most areas in the hub area that missed them, finishing the main level and the hidden island, as well as adding battle arenas, that unlock objects after the player defeats all of the enemies. I also fixed a small issue with the knockback, exiting the state after a short amount of time if the player doesn't touch the ground first. I then started planning the design of the final boss, and added homing cannons and an object rotator for it. I then added an enterance to the final bosses arena that unlocks once the player gets 15 collectables.

Week 16

During Week 16 I worked on the final boss. To start, I changed how the homing cannon I previously added works, and then made the level for the final boss. for the last part of the level, I added barriers that move towards the player when activated, that have small holes the player must jump through, this has some issues that need to be fixed though. I also added a new button type for the final boss. When it is pressed, the previos section of the level is removed, the next section is loaded in and the boss moves to it's next location. I then added two new classes that ensure that objects in a scene that are destroyed and buttons that are activated stay destroyed/activated when the scene is reloaded.

Week 17

I spent Week 17 mainly cleaning up the game. I first finished the small climbing challenge under the hub area. Then, I added boundaries in each level to respawn the player if they fall. I spent the rest of the week fixing minor issues, issues with cannonball collision, enemies not respawning on death, etc, cleaning up the levels, ensuring checkpoints work, And finishing the UI by adding a main menu and the players health. I then added 4 more hidden challenges across the three main scenes.
